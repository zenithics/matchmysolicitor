Tribunal process guides

The employment tribunal system has its own procedure, its own paperwork and its own rhythm, and knowing what comes next removes a great deal of the fear. These guides walk through the process step by step: the mandatory ACAS early conciliation stage, the ET1 claim form, case management, disclosure, witness statements and the hearing itself, along with realistic timescales for each stage. They also tackle the question everyone asks: whether you actually need a solicitor, and where representation genuinely changes outcomes. Time limits are the single biggest trap, with most claims needing to be started within 3 months less one day, so if your deadline is close, skip the reading and start a free enquiry today.
