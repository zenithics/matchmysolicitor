Disputes our partner solicitors take on

Unfair dismissal

Dismissed without a fair reason or fair process, with 2+ years' service? You may have a strong claim, and strict time limits apply. A specialist will assess your case and act quickly.

More about unfair dismissal →

Constructive dismissal

If your employer's conduct left you no real choice but to resign, you may be able to claim as if you were dismissed. Timing and evidence are critical, get advice before you resign if you can.

More about constructive dismissal →

Workplace discrimination

Treated worse because of age, sex, race, disability, or pregnancy and maternity? Discrimination awards are uncapped, and no minimum service is required. These claims demand real specialists.
