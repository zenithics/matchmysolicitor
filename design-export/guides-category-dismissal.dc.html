Dismissal guides

Losing your job is one of the most stressful things that can happen, and the law around it is full of terms that sound similar but mean very different things. Unfair dismissal, wrongful dismissal, constructive dismissal and redundancy each have their own rules, their own time limits and their own remedies. These guides explain each one in plain English: what the law actually says, who qualifies, what evidence matters and what you can realistically expect to recover. Start with the guide that matches your situation, and remember that most tribunal claims must be started within 3 months less one day of the dismissal.
