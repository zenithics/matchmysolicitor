Exit negotiations guides

Not every employment dispute ends in a tribunal. Many of the best outcomes are negotiated quietly: a fair settlement, an agreed reference, a clean exit on your terms. But negotiation has its own legal machinery, and phrases like "without prejudice", "protected conversation" and "garden leave" carry precise meanings that affect what can and cannot be used against you later. These guides explain the vocabulary and tactics of exit negotiations so you can recognise what is happening around you and avoid the common traps, like signing the first offer or saying too much in the wrong meeting.
