Why representation matters at this level

Senior packages are documents within documents: service agreements, share plan rules, bonus schemes, partnership deeds. What you are actually owed as a leaver depends on how these interact, and the leaver provisions are frequently more negotiable than they appear.

Your employer will take advice before the first conversation. Matching that early keeps the negotiation balanced, and it signals that the discussion will be conducted seriously and professionally.

Discretion is the discipline throughout. These negotiations are conducted quietly, usually while you remain in role, and are structured so that if no agreement is reached, nothing has been conceded and nothing has leaked.

Senior exit FAQs

My employer does not know I want to leave. Can I still get advice?

Yes, and that is the ideal time. A specialist can help you plan the approach, the timing and the opening position before anything is said internally.

How is a senior exit different from a settlement agreement?

The agreement is the final document. A senior exit is the negotiation that shapes it: bonus and equity treatment, restrictive covenants, references, announcements and timing, often over weeks of discreet discussion.

Will negotiating damage my relationship with the business?

Handled well, no. At senior level both sides usually want a clean, professional parting. Good advisers negotiate firmly on substance while keeping the tone constructive.

What about my equity, LTIPs and deferred bonus?

Leaver provisions are where senior exits are won and lost. Whether you are treated as a good leaver, and what happens to unvested awards, is often negotiable and can outweigh the headline payment.
