How it works

From enquiry to expert advice in three steps

Free, no obligation, and fast. Most people are speaking with their matched specialist within 24 hours.

1

Tell us your situation

Fill in our short enquiry form. Tell us whether you're an employer or an employee, what's happened, and how to reach you. In your own words, two paragraphs is fine. You don't need to know the legal name for your problem, and we don't need documents at this stage.

Everything you share is confidential and used only to match you with the right solicitor.

2

We match you with a vetted specialist

We review your enquiry and match it to a partner solicitor whose practice fits your dispute, tribunal defence, senior exits, discrimination, whatever it is. Every firm on our panel is SRA-regulated and vetted for genuine employment law specialism. No generalists, no call centres.

Urgent matter, like an interim relief hearing? Say so, and we prioritise same-day matching.

3

Get expert advice within 24 hours

Your matched solicitor contacts you directly for a free initial conversation about your situation and options. If you decide to instruct them, fees and next steps are agreed between you, clearly and upfront. If not, that's fine too. No obligation, ever.

Common questions

Does it cost anything to enquire?

No. The enquiry and the initial conversation with your matched solicitor are free. You only pay if you choose to instruct the solicitor, on terms you agree directly with them.

Are you a law firm?

No, we're a referral service. We don't give legal advice ourselves. Our job is to connect you with a vetted, SRA-regulated solicitor who specialises in your type of dispute.

How do you vet your partner solicitors?

Every partner firm must have a dedicated employment law practice, current SRA regulation, and a track record in contested employment matters. We review our panel regularly and remove firms that don't meet our standards.

What if my case isn't a good fit?

We'll tell you honestly. We connect people with solicitors for serious employment disputes, if what you need is free advice or a simple document review, we'll say so rather than waste your time.

Do you cover my area?

Yes, our panel covers the whole of the UK. Many matters can also be handled remotely by phone and video.
