Discrimination guides

The Equality Act 2010 protects you from being treated worse at work because of who you are: your age, sex, race, disability, or pregnancy and maternity, among other protected characteristics. Discrimination claims are different from ordinary dismissal claims in two important ways: there is no minimum length of service, and compensation is uncapped. But they are also legally technical, with different tests for direct discrimination, indirect discrimination, harassment and victimisation. These guides explain your rights for each protected characteristic, the evidence that matters, and the practical steps to take while you are still in the job.
