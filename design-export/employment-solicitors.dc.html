Common questions

Does my solicitor need to be local?

Rarely. Most of an employment dispute is handled by phone, video and email. Local knowledge still counts for how a particular tribunal lists and manages cases, which is why these pages exist, but it is not the main factor in a good match.

Do you cover areas outside these cities?

Yes. Our panel covers the whole of the UK, including Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. The city pages simply cover where the panel is most active.

What if I do not know what my claim is called?

That is normal and it does not matter. Describe what happened in your own words in the enquiry form. Putting the right legal label on it is the solicitor's job.
